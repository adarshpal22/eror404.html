About Our Project

Building a community where knowledge is shared.

📚 Community Library Exchange

BookBridge is a community library exchange website designed to encourage reading, book reuse, and knowledge sharing. Readers can explore a sample collection, learn how exchanges work, and submit a book request through the demonstration form.

Our goal is to make books more accessible, reduce unnecessary waste, and help students and readers discover new ideas through shared books.
